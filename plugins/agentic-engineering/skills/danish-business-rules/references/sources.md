Authorities whose guidance to read before deciding: the Consumer Ombudsman for marketing and
consumer contracts, the Agency for Digital Government for cookies, the Safety Technology
Authority for e-commerce under the accessibility act, the Business Authority for bookkeeping
and the tax authority for invoices. Their guidance is **Authority practice**. Apart from the
one page in the table, it was not summarised here.

## Not covered

This skill was written from the acts above and nothing else. It does not cover, and must not
be stretched to:

- the E-commerce Act's rules on information about the service provider. The act's text could
  not be read in the official database when this skill was written;
- the detailed price-labelling rules set under the Marketing Practices Act, section 14;
- environmental claims and sustainability labels in marketing, and the rest of what act
  558 of 2025 added from 27 September 2026 (repair scores, software updates, changes to
  what is shown just before the order);
- the VAT order's own text, when a simplified invoice is allowed, invoices to private
  customers, VAT rates, or sales to other
  countries;
- the accessibility requirements themselves (the act's annex) and public bodies' own
  accessibility law;
- sector rules: health, finance, food, alcohol, tobacco, gambling, travel;
- sales law: defects, guarantees, delivery, complaints, and the information about
  complaint bodies a shop has to give;
- payment rules, including fees for paying by card;
- anything outside Denmark, including where another country's consumer law applies to a
  Danish shop that sells abroad.

For each of these, say "not covered by this guidance" and escalate.

## Review plan

- **At every change to this skill**: re-read each source the changed text relies on, and
  update its date.
- **At least every six months**, next by 2027-05-11: open every link, confirm each
  consolidated act is still the one in force, look for amending acts, and re-read the tax
  authority's guide, which is reissued twice a year.
- **At once** when an act above is amended or consolidated again, or when someone reports a
  difference between the skill and a source. The source is right; the skill has a defect.
- **Who**: the repository that holds this skill's source. A review is recorded as a change to
  the dates in the table, with what was found.
