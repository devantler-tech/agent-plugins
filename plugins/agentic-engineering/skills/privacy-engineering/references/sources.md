## What this skill does not claim

It summarises. It does not interpret disputed points, it does not cover sector rules (health,
finance, telecoms), and it does not replace the accountable person or a lawyer. Where a
summary and the source differ, the source is right and the skill has a defect: report it.

## Review plan

- **At every change to this skill**: re-read each source the changed text relies on, and
  update its date.
- **At least every six months**: open every link, confirm the act's consolidated number is
  still the one in force, and read the agency's news for changed positions. The next review
  is due by 2027-05-10.
- **At once** when the Danish act is amended or consolidated again, when the European
  board publishes or replaces guidelines the skill relies on, or when someone reports a
  difference between the skill and a source.
- **Who**: the repository that holds this skill's source. A review is recorded as a change to
  the dates in the table, with what was found.
